What changed

No more research-chemical storefront.

The site is now structured as a healthcare information and access prototype. It does not offer investigational materials, research-use products, or direct-to-consumer prescription checkout.

What we can build around

Licensed clinician evaluation, approved-label education, patient-specific prescribing when appropriate, transparent pharmacy relationships, medication risk information, and follow-up care.

What stays off the site

Unapproved investigational products, research-use sales, dosing or reconstitution tutorials, injection instructions, hidden affiliate funnels, fabricated testing, and unsupported outcome claims.

Reliable source ≠ legal human-use product.

Product quality matters, but sourcing alone does not determine whether a drug can lawfully be marketed, prescribed, compounded, dispensed, or shipped to a patient.
