Clinical pathway

Prescription treatment is a care process, not an ecommerce checkout.

The site is being rebuilt around appropriate human-use care instead of direct product sales.

Step 1
Education

Patients receive balanced information about eligibility, approved uses, risks, alternatives, and what the care process involves.

Step 2
Clinical evaluation

An appropriately licensed clinician evaluates the patient and determines whether prescription treatment is medically appropriate.

Step 3
Prescription

A prescription is issued only when clinically appropriate and legally permitted in the patient’s state.

Step 4
Pharmacy fulfillment

Medication is dispensed by an appropriately licensed pharmacy. The site itself does not pretend to be the pharmacy.

FDA-approved medications

These are the preferred starting point when a suitable FDA-approved medication can meet the patient’s medical need. Any public description must stay accurate to the actual approved product and labeling.

Compounded medications

Compounded medications may serve individual patient needs in limited circumstances, but they are not FDA-approved. They must never be described as FDA-approved, FDA-generic, or automatically equivalent to an approved drug.

Launch rule: No named medication gets a public treatment page until its human-use pathway, provider relationship, pharmacy relationship, state availability, prescription requirements, claims, and required risk information have been verified.
