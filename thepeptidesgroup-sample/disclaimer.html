Research Use Terms

The research-use position applies to the entire relationship, not only to a label or checkbox.

Permitted purpose

Materials offered through the member catalog are intended solely for lawful laboratory, analytical, in-vitro and other non-clinical research by qualified research accounts.

Not permitted

Materials are not offered for human or veterinary administration, consumption, diagnosis, treatment, prevention, wellness, cosmetic, performance, body-composition or other personal use.

No use instructions

The site does not provide dosing, injection, reconstitution, cycling, administration, treatment or personal-use instructions. Questions seeking that information may result in the request or account being declined or reviewed.

Account certification

Applicants certify that their entity, role, address, research purpose and intended use are accurate. A research account may be reviewed or suspended when later information materially conflicts with the certified purpose.

Analytical documentation

COA, SDS, HPLC, LC-MS or other testing information applies only to the actual sample, method and lot documented by the supporting report. Analytical data does not establish clinical safety, efficacy, FDA approval or suitability for human or veterinary use.

Final legal review

This prototype language must be reviewed and finalized for the operating entity, jurisdictions, products, payment processor, shipping model and applicable law before production launch.
