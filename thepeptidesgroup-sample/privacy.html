Privacy Notice

Draft for privacy/legal review before production use.

Prototype status: the current static sample stores the research profile, session and cart in this browser only. It is not a production account system.

1. Research-account information

A production service may collect entity type, organization name, role, business or institutional contact information, address, research area, research model, research-purpose description, certifications and account history.

2. Order information

Production ordering may require purchaser identity, organization, shipping information, payment information through an authorized processor, order contents, lot references, research-use certifications and fulfillment records.

3. What should not be submitted

This research-account pathway is not a patient intake. Users should not submit diagnoses, medical history, prescriptions, personal treatment goals, dosing questions or other health information.

4. Account and compliance records

Records may be retained as reasonably necessary for account security, fraud prevention, research-use controls, order fulfillment, legal obligations, disputes and recordkeeping.

5. Service providers

Production vendors may include hosting, authentication, payment processing, fraud prevention, shipping, analytics and customer-support providers. Final disclosures will identify the actual categories and data practices used at launch.

6. Cookies and local storage

The prototype uses local browser storage for essential session, research-account, cart and privacy-choice functionality. Optional analytics should remain off until accepted where required.

7. Consumer privacy rights

Applicable state-specific disclosures, request methods, appeal rights and contact information will be added after the legal entity, service states and production data practices are finalized.
