Operating boundaries

The research position has to be real throughout the entire transaction.

A disclaimer is not the operating model. Public content, account application, catalog language, support responses and fulfillment all need to stay consistent with lawful non-clinical research.

1. Public content

Public pages focus on chemistry, analytical methods, documentation, research models and quality systems—not symptoms, treatment goals, personal outcomes or instructions for self-use.

2. Research account

Members provide their entity, role, business or institutional information, research area and research purpose, then certify that materials will not be used for human or veterinary administration.

3. Red flags

If an applicant or customer states a personal-use, treatment, dosing or administration intent, the research pathway should not simply ignore it. The prototype blocks common personal-use language in the application.

4. Product pages

Member pages may show identity, amount, price, lot status, COA/SDS status and analytical documentation. They do not provide dosing, reconstitution, injection, treatment or outcome guidance.

5. Testing claims

No HPLC, LC-MS, sterility, endotoxin, heavy-metal or other result is created from assumption. Results appear only when a real report supports the claim for the exact batch.

6. Order certification

The final order step repeats the non-clinical research certification and preserves the connection between member account, stated research purpose and purchased material.

Production requirement: this static sample demonstrates the user experience only. Real launch still requires server-side authentication, durable account records, order records, appropriate review rules, processor acceptance, final legal terms and a documented fulfillment policy.
