# Precision Patch — Customer Service SOP

## Scope
Customer service handles account access, order status, shipping, billing support, returns/refunds, damaged packages, and general product-information routing.

Customer service does **not** improvise medical, therapeutic, dosing, diagnosis, treatment, or outcome claims.

## Identity/account support
Before changing account/shipping information on an order, verify the customer through the production account/helpdesk process. Never request full payment-card numbers, CVV, passwords, or other secrets by email/chat.

## Approved support categories
- Account access/password reset.
- Order status.
- Address correction before fulfillment when possible.
- Tracking/delivery questions.
- Incorrect or damaged shipment.
- Refund/replacement status.
- Subscription cancellation/change if subscriptions are active.
- Directions/storage/ingredients only by quoting or linking the approved final label/Product Use & Safety information.

## Product questions
If the answer is not present in the approved product label, FAQ, or Product Use & Safety content:
1. Do not guess.
2. Record the question.
3. Escalate to the designated product/compliance owner.
4. Update approved source content only after verification.

## Prohibited support responses
Do not tell customers that the product:
- treats, cures, heals, prevents, or mitigates disease;
- causes weight loss, anti-aging, recovery, hormone, performance, or other medical/body-function outcomes unless specifically cleared and supported;
- is equivalent to an approved drug or therapy;
- is safe because of a testimonial, influencer, anecdote, or supplier assertion;
- should be used contrary to the approved label.

## Safety/product complaint
If a customer reports a reaction, injury, unexpected effect, or product-integrity concern:
1. Do not diagnose or provide treatment advice.
2. Advise the customer to stop using a damaged/compromised product where the approved safety information requires that.
3. If urgent medical symptoms are described, direct the customer to appropriate emergency/medical care rather than attempting clinical support.
4. Capture order number, product/lot if available, description, date, photos where relevant, and contact information.
5. Mark `PRODUCT_COMPLAINT` or `SAFETY_ESCALATION`.
6. Escalate under the final adverse-event/product-complaint procedure once established for the actual product.

## Refund/replacement workflow
1. Confirm order.
2. Confirm reason code.
3. Obtain photos when required by policy.
4. Apply published Returns & Refunds Policy.
5. Record refund/replacement decision, amount, date, and agent/system identity.
6. Never promise a processor posting time beyond the published estimate.

## Chargeback prevention
- Make merchant descriptor clear.
- Send order and shipping confirmations.
- Respond promptly to customer contacts.
- Make cancellation/refund rules easy to find.
- Preserve order, consent, payment, tracking, support, and refund records.

## Subscription support
If recurring plans are activated:
- provide a direct cancellation path;
- show next billing date and amount where feasible;
- do not create unnecessary cancellation friction;
- preserve cancellation timestamp and confirmation.

## Escalation levels
**L1 — Routine:** account, order, tracking, published policy questions.

**L2 — Operations:** refund exception, fulfillment failure, suspected fraud, repeat delivery issue.

**L3 — Compliance/Product:** safety complaint, claim question, label discrepancy, processor inquiry, regulator/legal inquiry.

## Pending before production
- Support platform/inbox.
- Support email/phone.
- Refund authority limits.
- Escalation contacts.
- Final product complaint/adverse-event process.
- Approved FAQ/macros based on the final product packet.
