Privacy Policy

Draft for final privacy/legal review before production launch.

1. Information we collect

Depending on the production services used, we may collect identifiers and account information such as name, email, phone number, login credentials, billing and shipping address; transaction and order information; customer-service communications; device/browser information; cookie and preference data; and information voluntarily submitted through forms.

2. Information we intentionally avoid collecting

The consumer product-intake flow should not collect medical history, diagnoses, prescriptions or other sensitive health information unless a future service actually requires it and the appropriate privacy/security framework is implemented first.

3. How we use information

We may use information to create and secure accounts, process orders and payments, fulfill and track shipments, provide support, prevent fraud, maintain records, comply with law, improve the site, measure performance, and send communications where permitted.

4. Service providers and third parties

Production vendors may include payment processors, fraud-prevention services, ecommerce/hosting providers, authentication services, shipping carriers, fulfillment providers, email/SMS platforms, customer-support tools, analytics providers and advertising platforms. These providers may process data for the services they perform. The final policy must identify the actual practices in use at launch.

5. Advertising and pixels

Optional analytics, advertising pixels, audience matching and remarketing should remain disabled until configured consistently with applicable consent and privacy requirements. Privacy choices must be honored across supported technologies.

6. Cookies and local storage

Essential technologies may support account sessions, security, cart state, preferences and checkout. Optional technologies should be controlled through the privacy-choice interface where required.

7. Retention and security

We retain information for legitimate business, transaction, fraud-prevention, legal and dispute purposes and use reasonable administrative, technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the data and services used.

8. State privacy rights

Residents of certain states may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, portability, or information about certain disclosures or uses of personal information, and to opt out of certain sale/sharing or targeted-advertising activities where applicable. Production request methods and verification procedures will be added before launch.

9. Children

The service is not directed to children. The purchase flow requires an adult-age confirmation.
