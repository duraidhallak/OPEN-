Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026.

Information we collect

We may collect name, email, phone number, account credentials, billing and shipping address, age or adult-status confirmation, state, order information, customer-service communications, device and browser information, cookie identifiers, and communication preferences.

Information we minimize

The consumer intake is not designed as a medical intake. We do not intend to request diagnoses, medical records, prescription history, insurance information, or detailed health histories unless a future service genuinely requires that information and the appropriate privacy and security framework is implemented first.

How we use information

We may use information to provide and secure accounts, process and verify orders, authorize payments, prevent fraud, fulfill and track shipments, communicate with customers, provide support, maintain transaction records, administer returns and refunds, comply with law, and improve the site.

Payments

Payment-card information will be processed by an authorized payment processor. We do not intend to store full card numbers or security codes on our own server.

Service providers

We may use vendors for hosting, authentication, ecommerce, payments, fraud prevention, email or SMS, customer support, fulfillment, shipping, analytics, security, and professional services. Those providers may process information to perform services for us.

Advertising and analytics

Optional advertising pixels, audience matching, targeted-advertising tools, and nonessential analytics will remain disabled until intentionally configured with the disclosures and privacy controls required for the actual practices.

Cookies and local storage

Essential technologies may support account sessions, security, cart state, checkout, site preferences, and privacy choices. Nonessential technologies will be controlled through the site privacy-choice mechanism where applicable.

Retention and security

We retain information only as reasonably necessary for fulfillment, support, fraud prevention, accounting, tax, legal, dispute, safety, and recordkeeping needs. We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information and services in use.

State privacy rights

Depending on where you live and which laws apply, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of personal information; learn about certain disclosures; opt out of certain sale, sharing, or targeted-advertising activities; or appeal a denied request. We will provide applicable request methods before production launch.

Notice at collection

At or before collection we will provide notice of the categories of information collected and the purposes for which they are used. If our practices materially change, the notice and this policy will be updated before the new collection or use begins.

Children

The storefront is not directed to children. Purchasing requires adult eligibility as specified at checkout.

Changes

We may update this policy when our technology, vendors, business practices, or legal obligations change. The current effective date will appear above.
