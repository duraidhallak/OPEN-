## Business model
Direct-to-consumer wearable patch storefront.

Flow:
1. Customer creates account.
2. Customer completes short product acknowledgement/intake.
3. Customer reviews finished-product listing and policies.
4. Customer places order.
5. Authorized processor accepts payment after underwriting approval.
6. Order is fulfilled and tracked.

No research-use positioning is used in the consumer storefront. The intake is not presented as a medical evaluation. A clinician or prescription workflow is not represented unless actually required and implemented for the exact finished product.

## Claims/marketing controls
The merchant description and public marketing must match the actual product and business. Do not:
- misclassify the product;
- conceal ingredients or required product information from underwriting;
- make therapeutic, disease, healing, recovery, weight-loss, anti-aging, hormone, performance, or other medical claims without a lawful basis and adequate support;
- use affiliates, testimonials, blogs, influencers, or customer-service scripts to make claims the company cannot make directly;
- submit a merchant description that differs materially from the actual storefront or fulfillment model.
