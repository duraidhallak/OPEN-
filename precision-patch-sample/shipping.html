Shipping Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026. Exact carrier, processing window, and service area will be inserted before launch.

Where we ship

Orders will be shipped only to destinations where the finished product may lawfully be sold and where our fulfillment and carrier partners support delivery. The final eligible states, territories, and countries will be published before payment is enabled.

Order processing

Orders may require payment authorization, fraud review, inventory confirmation, address validation, and other routine checks before fulfillment. The final published processing window will reflect the actual fulfillment operation in use at launch.

Shipping charges

Available shipping methods and charges will be disclosed before an order is submitted. Taxes, duties, or import charges, if applicable, will be handled as stated at checkout and under applicable law.

Tracking

When tracking is available, it will be sent using the contact information associated with the order. Tracking and carrier delivery estimates are informational and are not guaranteed delivery dates.

Address accuracy

Customers are responsible for providing a complete and accurate delivery address. Contact support immediately if a correction is required. Address changes cannot be guaranteed after an order enters fulfillment.

Failed or returned delivery

If a shipment is returned because of an incorrect or incomplete customer-provided address, refusal, or another delivery issue outside our control, reshipment or refund treatment will depend on the condition of the returned product, carrier charges, and applicable law.

Delays

Carrier, weather, operational, holiday, and other delays may occur after shipment. If tracking is materially delayed, contact support so we can review available carrier information and determine the appropriate next step.

Lost shipments

If tracking indicates a shipment may be lost, contact support promptly. We may open a carrier investigation and, based on the facts and applicable policy, provide a replacement, refund, or other resolution.

Delivered but not received

If tracking shows delivered but the package cannot be located, first check the delivery location, household members, neighbors where appropriate, and any carrier delivery notes. Contact support promptly if the package remains missing.

Damaged or opened packages

Do not use a product if its individual package is materially damaged, opened, contaminated, or altered. Contact support promptly with the order number and clear photos of the shipping package and product package. Retain the product and packaging until the claim is resolved.
