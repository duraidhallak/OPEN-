Shipping Policy

Draft fulfillment policy. Exact carrier, processing windows and service areas will be finalized before launch.

Where we ship

Production shipping destinations will be limited to jurisdictions where the finished product may lawfully be sold and where our carriers and fulfillment partners support delivery. The final state/country list will be published before payment is enabled.

Processing time

Orders may require payment, fraud, inventory, address or compliance review before shipment. The actual published fulfillment window will be inserted after the fulfillment operation is confirmed.

Tracking and delivery

When tracking is available, it will be sent to the customer using the contact information associated with the order. Carrier estimates are not guaranteed delivery dates.

Incorrect addresses

Customers are responsible for providing a complete and accurate shipping address before fulfillment. Contact support immediately if an address correction is needed; changes cannot be guaranteed after an order enters fulfillment.

Delayed or lost shipments

Customers should contact support when tracking is materially delayed or shows a delivery issue. We may work with the carrier and determine replacement or refund eligibility based on the facts, carrier records and applicable law.

Damaged packages

Do not use a materially damaged, opened or altered product package. Contact support promptly with the order number and photos so the issue can be reviewed.
