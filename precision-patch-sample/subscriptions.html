Recurring Orders & Subscriptions

Last updated: August 26, 2026. No recurring plan is active in the current prototype.

Enrollment

If recurring orders are offered, enrollment will require an affirmative customer selection. The checkout will disclose the product, recurring price, billing frequency, shipping frequency, renewal terms, and cancellation method before the customer enrolls.

Recurring charges

By enrolling in a recurring plan, a customer authorizes the disclosed recurring charges until cancellation takes effect. Any material change to price or billing terms will be handled with the notice and consent required by applicable law.

Cancellation

Customers will be provided a reasonably simple cancellation method. The final production process will identify the cutoff for stopping the next recurring order. Cancellation stops future renewals but may not reverse an order already accepted for fulfillment.

Failed payments

If a recurring payment fails, we may retry the payment where permitted, request updated payment information, pause the recurring plan, or cancel it.

Returns and refunds

Recurring orders remain subject to the Returns & Refunds Policy. Cancellation of a recurring plan does not create a separate refund right for an order already fulfilled or shipped.

Reminders and notices

Pre-renewal, price-change, or other notices will be provided when required by applicable law or the terms of the selected plan.
