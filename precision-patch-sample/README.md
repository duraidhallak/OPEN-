# Precision Patch consumer prototype

Working consumer storefront kept separate from `thepeptidesgroup-sample/`.

## Flow
Public site → account → product acknowledgement → product → order review → payment (disabled).

## Non-negotiable launch gates
- Final brand/legal entity.
- Exact finished-product regulatory classification.
- Complete, accurate ingredient/identity disclosure wherever legally required.
- Final package/label, directions, warnings, lot/expiration/manufacturer information.
- Finished-product quality/safety/testing documents.
- Payment-processor/acquirer pre-underwriting for the exact product and website.
- Product liability insurance and fulfillment details.
- Qualified legal review of Terms, Privacy, Shipping, Returns/Refunds, Product Use & Safety.

## Marketing boundary
Do not add therapeutic, disease, weight-loss, anti-aging, recovery, hormone, performance or other medical claims unless specifically lawful and substantiated for the exact finished product.

Do not use research-use-only language on this consumer human-use storefront.

Do not remove, cover, alter or obscure required product identity, ingredient, warning, lot, expiration, manufacturer/distributor or other legally required label information.

## Prototype limitations
The account/intake/cart are browser-local demos only. No server authentication, database, payment processing, order transmission, analytics pixels or real fulfillment are connected.
