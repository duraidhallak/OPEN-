Returns & Refunds Policy

Draft policy. Final unopened-product and time-window rules depend on packaging, product stability and fulfillment operations.

Order cancellations

Contact support promptly to request cancellation. Cancellation cannot be guaranteed after an order has entered fulfillment or shipped.

Incorrect orders

If we shipped the wrong item or quantity, contact support with the order number and package photos. Verified errors will be corrected through replacement or refund as appropriate.

Damaged or compromised product

Do not use a product if its individual package is materially damaged, open, contaminated or altered. Contact support promptly with photos and retain the product/package until instructed otherwise.

Customer returns

For safety and integrity reasons, opened or used wearable products should not be restocked. The final policy for unopened returns, return authorization and applicable time limits will be published before launch.

Refund timing

Approved refunds will be issued to the original payment method. Financial institutions may require additional time to post the credit.

Chargebacks

Customers should contact support first so genuine fulfillment, damage or billing problems can be investigated. Nothing in this policy limits legal rights that cannot be waived.
