Returns & Refunds Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026. Final time windows and unopened-product rules will be set from the actual packaging and fulfillment model before launch.

Order cancellations

Contact support as soon as possible if you need to cancel an order. We will attempt to stop fulfillment when practical, but cancellation cannot be guaranteed after an order has entered packing or shipment.

Incorrect orders

If we send the wrong product or quantity, contact support promptly with the order number and clear photos of the shipping package and product received. Verified fulfillment errors will be corrected through replacement, refund, or another appropriate remedy.

Damaged or compromised product

Do not use a product if its individual package is materially damaged, opened, contaminated, altered, or otherwise compromised. Contact support promptly and retain the product and packaging until the claim is resolved.

Unopened returns

The final eligibility window for unopened returns will be published before launch. Any eligible return must be authorized before shipment and must be received in the condition required by the final return instructions.

Opened or used product

For product-integrity and consumer-safety reasons, opened, used, or materially altered wearable products generally cannot be returned for resale. This does not limit rights or remedies that apply to defective, incorrect, damaged, or otherwise nonconforming goods under applicable law.

Return shipping

If a return is approved because of our verified error or a qualifying defect, return-shipping treatment will follow the instructions provided by support. For discretionary customer returns, final responsibility for return shipping will be stated in the launch version of this policy.

Refunds

Approved refunds will be issued to the original payment method unless another method is required by law. Banks and card issuers may require additional time to post a credit after we submit it.

Replacements

Where appropriate, we may offer a replacement instead of a refund for a verified damaged, incorrect, or compromised shipment. Replacement availability depends on inventory and shipping eligibility.

Subscriptions

If recurring orders are later offered, cancellation of a future recurring order must be completed before the applicable billing or fulfillment cutoff disclosed at enrollment. Canceling a subscription does not automatically reverse an order that has already been accepted for fulfillment.

Chargebacks and billing questions

Customers may contact support for billing, fulfillment, damage, or refund issues so we can investigate and provide available records. Nothing in this policy limits a consumer right that cannot legally be waived.

Abuse and fraud

We may decline refund or replacement requests involving material fraud, falsified evidence, abusive return activity, or other misuse, subject to applicable law and payment-network requirements.
