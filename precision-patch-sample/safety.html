Product Use & Safety

Last updated: August 26, 2026. This page is intentionally limited to product integrity, label-following, and consumer-safety information.

Launch gate: final ingredients, amounts, directions, warnings, contraindications, manufacturer or distributor information, lot controls, expiration, storage requirements, and adverse-event contact information must be inserted from the reviewed finished-product documentation before payment is enabled.

Read the complete finished-product label

Customers should receive the product with all legally required identity, ingredient, direction, warning, lot, expiration, and manufacturer or distributor information intact and legible. Required information should not be removed, altered, covered, or obscured.

Use only as labeled

Use directions will be published only after the final product label has been reviewed. Website instructions, packaging, customer-service scripts, and marketing materials must remain consistent with the finished-product label and supporting documentation.

Package integrity

Do not use a patch if the individual package is materially damaged, open, contaminated, altered, or expired. Contact support if a shipment arrives in questionable condition.

Skin-use information

Product-specific application-area, wear-time, skin-sensitivity, removal, and discontinuation instructions will be added only from the validated label and safety documentation for the exact finished product.

Storage

Storage and temperature instructions will be published from the final product documentation. Customers should follow the instructions on the finished-product package and should not use product that has been stored under conditions that materially conflict with those instructions.

No unsupported claims

The website, product pages, advertisements, email, SMS, social media, affiliates, testimonials, customer-service scripts, and other communications must not make therapeutic, disease, body-function, performance, or other claims unless those claims are legally permitted and adequately supported for the exact finished product.

No disguised claims

We do not use testimonials, affiliates, blogs, social posts, influencers, or other third parties to communicate claims that would be inappropriate or unsupported if stated directly by the company.
