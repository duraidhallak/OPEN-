Cookie & Tracking Technologies Notice

Last updated: August 26, 2026.

Essential technologies

Essential cookies and local-storage technologies may be used for account sessions, security, fraud prevention, cart state, checkout, site preferences, and remembering privacy choices. These technologies support core site functions.

Optional analytics

Nonessential analytics will remain disabled until intentionally configured. If enabled, the site will identify the applicable categories of analytics technology and provide consent or opt-out controls where required.

Advertising technologies

Advertising pixels, audience matching, cross-site tracking, and targeted-advertising technologies are not part of the current prototype. Before any such technology is activated, the Privacy Policy and Privacy Choices interface will be updated to reflect the actual practice.

Your choices

The site may provide controls for essential-only use and for optional technologies. Browser settings may also allow you to block or delete cookies, although disabling essential technologies can affect site functionality.

Changes

This notice will be updated when the technologies actually used by the production storefront change.
