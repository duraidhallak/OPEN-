# Precision Patch — Standing Compliance Guardrails

These rules apply to the website, product pages, checkout, email, SMS, social media, affiliates, influencers, customer-support scripts, packaging drafts, and processor underwriting materials.

## Public positioning
- Consumer patch brand, not a research-material storefront.
- Do not use peptide-oriented marketing or SEO as the public acquisition strategy.
- Do not make disease, treatment, cure, healing, recovery, weight-loss, anti-aging, hormone, performance, or other therapeutic/body-function claims unless the exact finished product has a lawful basis for that claim and adequate support.
- Do not imply clinical outcomes through testimonials, before/after stories, affiliates, blogs, influencers, or indirect wording.
- Do not disguise company-controlled marketing as independent customer experience or independent editorial content.

## Product integrity
- Website information must match the final finished-product label and supporting documentation.
- Do not invent ingredient amounts, test results, purity, stability, delivery performance, safety results, manufacturer credentials, certifications, regulatory status, or customer outcomes.
- Required product identity, ingredients, warnings, directions, lot, expiration, and manufacturer/distributor information must remain accurate and legible wherever required.
- Do not remove, cover, alter, or obscure required label information.

## Consumer flow
- Current intended flow: account → short product acknowledgement/intake → order → payment → fulfillment.
- Do not make the intake look like a medical evaluation unless a licensed clinical service is actually added and legally structured.
- Collect only information needed for the consumer transaction and product-use acknowledgements.

## Payments and underwriting
- Merchant processors and acquiring banks must receive an accurate description of the actual finished product and business model.
- Do not activate production card processing until the exact product, label, fulfillment model, claims, and merchant category have been reviewed by the selected processor/acquirer.
- Do not misclassify the product, hide ingredients, or use a misleading merchant description to obtain approval.

## Privacy and marketing technology
- Optional analytics and advertising pixels remain off until actual vendors and privacy controls are configured.
- Privacy disclosures must match actual data practices.
- Marketing email/SMS requires the applicable consent and unsubscribe process.

## Launch gates
Before production payment is enabled, confirm:
1. Final legal entity and contact information.
2. Final consumer brand/name clearance.
3. Final packaging and label.
4. Exact ingredient and amount information required for the finished product.
5. Manufacturer/distributor documentation.
6. Finished-product testing actually available.
7. Directions, warnings, storage, expiration, and product-use information.
8. Shipping and fulfillment model.
9. Refund/return operational rules.
10. Processor/acquirer pre-underwriting and approval.
11. Final legal review of jurisdiction-specific terms and product classification.

These guardrails are intentionally conservative so later design or marketing work does not recreate the contradictions identified during the prior regulatory review.
